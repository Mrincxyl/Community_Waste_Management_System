The challenge

The problem we wanted to solve

In many areas, waste collects on roadsides, near markets, and in open spaces. Residents see it every day, but reporting it is often inconvenient and the aftermath is rarely visible.

We began this project in February 2026 as part of a Django internship. Rather than building a basic practice app, we chose a real operational problem: how to make waste reporting easy for the public and more manageable for the teams responsible for cleanup.

WasteTracker is our way of closing that gap — giving citizens a simple way to submit issues, and giving officers and workers a structured path to review, assign, and close problems with accountability.

Workflow

How the process works

1

A resident submits a report

With a photo, location, and the details of the issue.

2

An officer reviews it

Confirms the details and assigns the job to a field worker.

3

The worker completes the task

Status is updated until the issue is recorded as resolved.
